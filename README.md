# Trucks Up: Truck & Load Management (Flutter)

**Truck and load management with live GPS tracking on Google Maps, trips with route and status, and trip records ready for invoicing.**

Built by [Oriental Tech Solutions](https://github.com/OrientalTechSolutions) · Flutter

---

## The need

A transport business needed to see every truck on the map, manage loads and trips in one place, and keep trip records ready for invoicing.

## What we built

- **Live GPS tracking** of every truck on Google Maps
- **Fleet list:** each truck with its status (active, on route, in maintenance, inactive) and driver
- **Load management:** create, assign and follow loads
- **Trips** with route, ETA and status
- **Trip records** ready for invoicing

## Tech stack

`Flutter` `Dart` `Google Maps` `Live GPS` `REST API`

## Our role

App development, maps and live tracking, load and trip workflows, testing and release.
